# Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** April 20, 2026

## Overview
GroceryList ("the App") is a personal grocery and meal planning app. Your privacy is important to us. This policy explains what data we collect and how it is used.

## Data We Collect
- **Grocery items, recipes, and meal plans** you create in the app
- **iCloud account identifier** — used solely to sync your data across your own devices via CloudKit

## How Your Data Is Used
- Your data is stored on your device and optionally synced to your private iCloud account using Apple's CloudKit
- We do not sell, share, or transmit your data to any third parties
- We do not use any third-party analytics or advertising SDKs

## Data Storage
- All data is stored locally on your device and in your personal iCloud account
- We have no access to your iCloud data — it is private to you

## Children's Privacy
The App does not knowingly collect data from children under 13.
